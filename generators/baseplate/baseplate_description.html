Description

Generate a baseplate to hold your bins, from a minimal frame to a fully-featured plate: solid, skeletonized (plastic-saving), or weighted (with underside pockets for steel/sand), plus optional magnet holes that line up with the bins' magnets and countersunk screw holes for mounting the plate to a surface.

Parameters
  • Size: Width and Length of the baseplate in grid units (1 unit = 42mm)
  • Base: The style of the plate below the grid frame (frame only, solid, skeletonized or weighted) and its thickness
  • Magnets & screws: Add magnet holes at each cell corner and/or countersunk mounting screw holes at each cell center
  • Other: Select the output format (STL or STEP)

Baseplate style

Selects what sits underneath the grid frame:

  • Frame only — just the click-in grid profile with nothing below it. Fastest print, least plastic.
  • Solid — the frame on a solid slab. Needed for magnet or screw holes, and stiffer on uneven surfaces.
  • Skeletonized — a solid slab with a large round cutout in each cell. Nearly as stiff as solid while saving plastic and print time. Magnet corners are kept.
  • Weighted — a thicker slab with a pocket opening from the top of each cell. Drop in steel plate, washers or BBs — the bins sitting on the plate cover the pockets. The underside stays completely flat, so the plate prints without supports.

Base thickness sets the height of the slab below the frame. It is automatically increased when needed (e.g. magnet holes need at least 2.4 mm, and weighted plates need room for the pocket and its floor).

Selecting magnet or screw holes with Frame only automatically switches the plate to Solid, since the holes need material to sit in.

Screw holes are countersunk mounting holes in the center of each cell for fastening the plate to a surface. They are available on Solid and Weighted plates (on weighted plates the screw head is recessed into the pocket floor). Skeletonized plates remove the center material, so screw holes are not available there.
